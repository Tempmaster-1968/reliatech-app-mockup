# ReliaTech

A phone-app mockup for ReliaTech Services (Long Beach HVAC). Sample data only — no real customer or job records.

Open this on an iPhone in Safari (no account or sign-in):

**https://tempmaster-1968.github.io/reliatech-app-mockup/**

## Add to Home Screen (iPhone)

1. Open the link in Safari (not Chrome).
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**. The app opens full screen with the ReliaTech icon.

## What’s in the mockup

Jobs, customers, money, and capture tabs; color-coded job cards; a three-step New call flow; cooling and heating readings with compare and Job Link screenshot sample; a flat-rate price book and estimate preview. Everything stays on the phone — nothing is saved to a server.

Icons, rates, and names are sample only. Real prices would come from Steve’s Price Books in Drive.
